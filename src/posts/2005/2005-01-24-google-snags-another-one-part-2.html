Ben Goodger, the lead developer of Mozilla Firefox is now employed by Google.

What does this mean for Firefox? Probably not much since he is still working (“devoted”) full time with Firefox, he’s just gettting paid from Google instead of the Mozilla Foundation.

What does this mean for Google? This is probably bigger but who knows. All we know is that they are hiring all the smartest people and have hired key persons from the IE team as well as from the Avalon team.

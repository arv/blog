I’ve udpated the PNG Behavior to work better when printing. I knew that the PNG images were not being printed but I thought it was just a matter of adding a print style sheet but IE is not detaching the behavior when printing so I had to revert to the IE proprietary events, window.onbeforeprint and window.onafterprint to turn off the filter and set the real image src.

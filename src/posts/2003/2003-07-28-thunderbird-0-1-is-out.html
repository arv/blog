Thunderbird, the standalone Mozilla Mail client, has reached its first milestone. I’ve been using this since day one and it is very stable (and it is only at 0.1)

This is the perfect mail client for anyone tired of spam, tired of OE’s lagging IMAP support, or just plain tired of Outlook’s bloat. Of course it is not perfect but considering that it is only at 0.1 and it has already passed all of the major email applications the future for Thunderbird looks bright indeed.
